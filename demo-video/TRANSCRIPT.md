# Demo video narration

## 00:00:00 — PA & TA Application Automation

Professional and Teaching Assistant recruitment involves applications, academic checks, reviews, and final lists. Manual coordination makes this process harder to manage.

## 00:00:10 — From manual coordination to a shared workflow

Our solution brings the complete workflow into one portal, from generated application forms to approved results.

## 00:00:16 — Three workspaces. One application cycle.

Administrators configure and approve. Students apply and track progress. Faculty review candidates for their assigned courses.

## 00:00:24 — Monitor the active semester

The Admin dashboard shows the active semester, application counts, filled positions, and vacancies.

## 00:00:30 — Configure positions and application dates

Admin sets course slots, assistantship amounts, application dates, and course-head assignments.

## 00:00:36 — Publish eligibility to generate forms

Publishing year, C G P A, grade, and interview rules generates the opportunities and forms.

## 00:00:42 — Start with the academic profile

The student starts with a preloaded academic profile, reducing repeated data entry.

## 00:00:47 — Discover eligible PA and TA opportunities

Opportunities show eligibility, available positions, requirements, and assistantship amounts. The backend rechecks eligibility before submission.

## 00:00:56 — Submit a prefilled application

This form already contains academic details. The student only adds an optional statement.

## 00:01:02 — Track multiple applications

Here, two P A applications and one T A application are submitted and tracked separately.

## 00:01:07 — Faculty access follows course assignments

Faculty can access only their assigned active-semester course. Let us follow the review process.

## 00:01:13 — Review the submitted academic snapshot

Faculty inspect the submitted academics, statement, and timetable. Private notes support their review.

## 00:01:20 — Shortlist and select within capacity

The candidate is shortlisted and selected. Capacity checks prevent the course from being overfilled.

## 00:01:26 — Accept the PA appointment

Accepting the P A appointment automatically cancels other pending applications for the same role.

## 00:01:31 — Follow the required TA interview branch

For the interview-required T A role, faculty schedule an interview with its time and location.

## 00:01:37 — Record evaluation before TA selection

An evaluation must be recorded before selection. The T A appointment is then accepted.

## 00:01:42 — Submit both appointments to Admin

One student may hold one P A and one T A. Faculty submit both appointments to Admin.

## 00:01:48 — Review submissions and finalize

Admin reviews submitted appointments and conflicts, then finalizes the semester, locking the approved results.

## 00:01:54 — Automatically generate the final selected list

The final selected list is generated automatically and can be downloaded as Excel or P D F.

## 00:02:00 — See automatic cancellation and accepted roles

The student can see the other P A application automatically cancelled, with both accepted roles retained.

## 00:02:06 — View personal approved results

Personal final results show only that student's approved appointments and amounts.

## 00:02:10 — A full-stack system with server-enforced rules

React and Vite provide the frontend. Express enforces access and workflow rules. PostgreSQL transactions protect appointments; local mode uses P G Lite.

## 00:02:20 — University integration with a defined contract

Revocable sessions protect access. An optional signed intranet interface supports institutional identity and academic synchronization.

## 00:02:28 — Less coordination. Clearer decisions.

The expected impact is less manual entry, organized reviews, clearer status tracking, and auditable final lists.

## 00:02:35 — Ready to demonstrate. Next: university rollout.

This demonstration uses synthetic data. Real university sign-in and production hosting remain to configure; duty timetable-overlap detection is a future extension.
